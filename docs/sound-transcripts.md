# Sound transcripts

Machine transcripts (faster-whisper small.en) of the select ("what") lines of the generated presets and of the faction done lines, used to pick question lines for `permission-asked` and done lines for `work-complete`. Stylized voices mis-transcribe often (Protoss and Zerg lines, invented languages), and growls come out as noise or hallucinations such as "Thanks for watching!", so listen before relying on a row.
